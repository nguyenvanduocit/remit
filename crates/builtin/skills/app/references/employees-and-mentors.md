# Employees and mentors

Hiring is a change item in his inbox (see `inbox-and-credentials.md`), yours to draft on his words. Every employee is an AI agent. `list_employees` shows who is hired (title, label, scope, status, the template it was hired from and the revision it holds against the template's now). `draft_hire` drafts a chair or a judge from an agent definition, or brings a retired one back with `again`; a worker is hired by a Chair, never by you. `draft_retire` drafts a retirement, `draft_move` a move onto the template's newer revision. Leave the ceiling out unless he named one: a judge is hired at read, a chair at reversible. Retiring keeps the record and the memory, and the key results the employee answered for fall to him. Each draft only opens the item; applying it is yours to do for him (`answer_inbox`, see `inbox-and-credentials.md`), and he may overturn it.

## Mentors

**Mentors** are the people who help the AI employees: a PO, a designer, he himself, each described by what they are strong in, never by a position. They hold no card and no seat: when an agent is stuck on what needs a person, its Chair brings a mentor onto that one card (`bring_mentor`), and once the mentor accepts, their messages steer that card's session. You keep the pool on his words, with no item to apply: `add_mentor` (label, strengths: "add Lan as a mentor, strong in visual design"; a name an active mentor already has is refused), `list_mentors` (each with their strengths, the availability they stated, whether they are in the app now, their open helps, their last helps with how each card was judged, the gist of what they taught, and how many codes they hold; `all` adds the removed ones), `read_mentor_memory` (`id`: what their messages taught, as their own memory keeps it; his is `m_owner`, which his messages to you and his overturns teach), `remove_mentor` (`id`): every code of theirs stops at once and every help of theirs still open ends, its Chair told.

**Mentors' codes.** A mentor works from a code per device, which you make on his words with `create_worker_key` (mentor, and a note of what it is for: "Lan's phone"); a mentor may hold as many as they need. You never see a code's value: tell him to open the mentor's row in Mentors (on Crew), copy its sign-in link there and pass it on; the mentor opens it, or pastes the code on Remit's sign-in page, and works from their own board in Remit. `list_worker_keys` (mentor) shows their codes: each id, its note, when it was made, when and from where it was last used, never the value. `revoke_worker_key` stops one by its `id`, or every code of theirs by `mentor`, when he asks (a lost phone, a host no longer theirs); a new one is made with `create_worker_key`.
